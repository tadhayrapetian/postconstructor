# Module `stock` — «Склад» и себестоимость
Rail: group `sklad`, title «Склад» (icon: boxes), before `orders`, `screen:true` for the table/journal.
- **Материалы**: list linked to the app's paper types (`st.shop.prices.papers` ids/names/gsm, cost `c` per sheet) + other consumables with defaults: фотобумага глянец/мат 10×15 и A4, самоклеящаяся A4, плёнка для ламинирования A4/A5/10×15/визитка, пружины 6–12 мм, обложки, конверты, файлы, папки. Fields: name, unit (лист/шт/пачка=500), stock qty, min level, cost per unit, supplier, note. Add/edit/delete; quick «+пачка»/«−лист» buttons; **Приход** (qty + price → moving-average cost; checkbox «обновить себестоимость в ценах» → writes `c` into the matching `st.shop.prices.papers` item), **Списание** (брак/порча with reason), **Инвентаризация** (set actual qty → logs difference). Journal of movements with filters and CSV export.
- **Чернила**: for each printer — Canon GX4040 (bottles GI-56: PGBK, C, M, Y) and Canon PIXMA G3410 (GI-490: PGBK, C, M, Y) — verify bottle volumes and ISO page yields from your knowledge/WebSearch and note them; show estimated level % per colour as nice bottle graphics (with the real ink colours), «Заправил» (set 100% / logs cost of the bottle), bottle prices editable, low-ink warnings.
- **Расчёт краски по макету**: render the current design side(s) to a small canvas (reuse the app's export rendering: find `pageCanvas`/`expPages` or similar; or `html2canvas` via `loadLib`) → convert pixels RGB→CMYK (simple GCR) → coverage % per C/M/Y/K → ml per page using ISO yield (yield pages at ~5% coverage per colour) → **cost of ink per sheet** and per item (divide by items per sheet from the app's imposition, find how the app computes per-sheet count). Show it as a card in the «Склад» screen and also as a compact line in the app's «Цена» (calc) panel («Краска ≈ 12 ֏/лист, бумага 35 ֏ → себестоимость…») by wrapping the calc render safely (only when the calc tab is active; never break it).
- **Автосписание** (toggle, default on): on `KS.on('print')` for non-test prints in Типография, deduct sheets of the current paper (count the printed sheets: pages in `#printRoot` after printNow, or the app's own sheet count) and ink by coverage × sheets for the current printer; log it; toast «Списано: 12 листов картона 300 г/м², краска ≈ 1,3 мл». Never double count on re-print of the same job without confirmation? → keep simple: each real print = write-off, with «Отменить списание» in the toast/journal.
- **Предупреждения**: low stock items → red badge with count on the «Склад» rail button and a one-time toast per day at boot; «Список закупок» (printable A4 / copy to clipboard as text) with quantities to reach a target level.
Tests: write-off after printNow (non-test) with the right paper and sheet count; test print doesn't write off; receipt changes average cost and optional price sync; inventory difference logged; ink estimate for a solid black card vs a white card (black ≫ white), CMYK sums plausible; calc panel shows the cost line and still works; badge; persistence and undo safety; screenshots light/dark/iPad.
