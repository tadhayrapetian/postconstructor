# Module `pos` — «Касса»
Rail: group `kassa`, groupTitle «Касса» (icon: cash register / banknote), before `orders`. Tabs: `seg:'Продажа'` (`screen:true`), `seg:'Смена'` (`screen:true`), `seg:'Услуги'` (price list editor in the panel, preview list on the screen).
Data: `KS.store('pos',{items:[…], sales:[], shifts:[], seq:{check:1,invoice:1,act:1}, ...})` — follow HOST.md "Money received".
- **Услуги/товары** catalogue with categories and sensible defaults for a copy & print shop in Armenia (prices in ֏, clearly editable; mark as «примерные цены»): Печать ч/б A4 (1 стор./2 стор.), Печать цветная A4 (текст/с фото/на всю страницу), Печать A3 (если есть), Ксерокопия ч/б/цвет, Скан (в файл/на почту), Распечатка с телефона/флешки, Ламинирование A4/A5/10×15/визитка, Брошюровка пружиной (до 50/100 листов), Резка, Фото 10×15/13×18/A4, Фото на документы, Визитки 100 шт (1/2 стороны), Листовки A5/A6 (10/50/100), Наклейки (лист A4), Набор текста (стр.), Дизайн (час), Отправка e-mail, Конверт, Папка, Файл-вкладыш. Item: `{id,cat,name,price,unit,cost?,fav,color?}`; add/edit/delete/reorder, import/export CSV.
- **Продажа** screen = POS: big touch tiles (favourites first, category filter, search), each tap adds to the cart; cart in the side panel with qty steppers, per-line price edit, discount (% or sum), client (pick from `st.clients` or quick new), payment method buttons Наличные / Карта / Перевод / В долг, «Получено» → сдача, «Оплатить» (big). Also «Оформить как заказ» → `newOrder` with the items in the note and price (for jobs that will be ready later), and «Предоплата».
- **Оплата заказов**: list of unpaid/partly paid orders (`price > paid`, status not cancel) with «Принять оплату» → amount + method → updates `order.paid` and records a sale `{type:'order',orderId}`.
- **Документы** (A4/A5 printable via `KS.print`, numbered, with shop requisites from `st.shop` name/phone/addr; editable shop requisites card: ИНН/ՀՎՀՀ (tax id), банк, счёт): Товарный чек, Квитанция (половина A4, 2 копии на листе), Счёт на оплату, Акт выполненных работ. Print right after payment (toggle «Печатать чек»).
- **Смена**: open shift with cash in drawer, внесение/изъятие (type in/out), list of today's operations, returns («Возврат» with reason → negative sale), **Z-отчёт** (close shift): totals by payment method, by category, number of checks, average check, refunds, expected cash in drawer vs counted (enter counted cash → difference). Print Z-report. History of shifts with reopenable reports. X-report (без закрытия).
- Listen to `KS.on('posAdd',({name,price,qty})=>…)` from other modules → adds a line to the cart and opens the Касса tab (`KS.show('kassa','sale')`).
- Keyboard on Mac: digits/Enter for quick amounts; touch-first on iPad.
Make it look like a real modern POS (big clear tiles with colours by category, large totals, satisfying feedback), light/dark.
Tests: sell items with each payment method, change calculation, discount, debt, order payment updates `order.paid`, refund, shift open/in/out/close → Z-report numbers exactly right, documents print capture contains correct totals and numbering increments, posAdd event, persistence across reload and project switch (shared), undo does not revert sales, screenshots (light/dark/iPad).
