# SlidePills

A centred phrase with its translation in a solid accent pill beneath.

**Use** for short phrases whose translation fits on one or two lines («Пилюли»).

**Consumer provides:** the tag, the phrase, the translation (goes in the pill), handle and counter.

- Phrase: Montserrat arm 900, 48px, `ink`. Pill: `--acc` fill (alternating `a0`, `a1`), `bg` text, Montserrat arm 700 at 30px, `radius-pill`, 16 × 34 padding.
- `bg` on `a1` can be low contrast (1.5:1 in the light reference). Check the pair, or keep the pill on `a0`.
