# SlideEditorial

A poster layout: two phrases per slide set large and heavy directly on the background, divided by `currentColor` rules.

**Use** for punchy, text-only carousels («Плакат»). No cards, no shadows.

**Consumer provides:** the tag, and per item the number, phrase and translation; the handle and the slide counter.

- Phrase is Montserrat arm 900 at 42px; translation `translation` at 28px, 85% opacity; numbers Atyan at 55% opacity.
- Items are split by a 2px rule; the footer sits on a 1px rule. Both use `currentColor` (`ink`).
- 76 × 74 inset.
